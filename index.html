The hour before sunrise, carried into any hour.

Many traditions keep the ninety-six minutes before dawn for practice. The air is still, the house is asleep, and the day has not started asking for anything yet.

Ekantik is made to carry that hour with you, into a crowded train, a long afternoon, the minute before a hard conversation.

No. Ekantik has no Bluetooth, no Wi-Fi and no app. Everything happens on the ring, and nothing leaves it.

Yes. Start with one breath practice a day. Each practice is one screen deep, so there is nothing to learn before you begin.
